How it works

A collar, delivered as a toggle. No options UI, no custody, no capital migration — protection attaches to the position the trader already holds.

01 · TOGGLE

Trader flips it on

We read the real position from your venue — side, size, mark. One tap is the entire user experience.

02 · STRUCTURE

The market builds the collar

We buy a protective put (~6% below spot) and sell a call above spot on listed options books. The call funds the put — the surplus, net of exchange fees, is the trader's credit.

03 · EARN

Credit vests, protection renews

The credit vests to the daily listed expiry and auto-renews at the morning book while the toggle stays on. Toggle off anytime — vested credit is kept.

If the market can't fund a credit, we refuse — and say why.

No manufactured yield. No subsidized APY. No silent size changes, ever. Real strikes, real fills, and every cent auditable to an exchange execution and reconciled against the venue's own settlement prints. In a market that's been burned by invented numbers, our refusals are the feature.

Built for the venue, priced by the market

Drawdowns are the number-one churn event on every trading platform. Protected traders survive them — and keep trading with you.

For exchanges & platforms

  • Retention where it hurts: the traders most likely to churn are the ones a drawdown just hit. The wrap is the countermeasure.
  • Volume stays on venue: unlike LP and staking yield, protection doesn't park capital — it keeps positions open and trading.
  • Your brand, your UX: a native toggle or a simple API. The trader never leaves your screen.
  • Simple economics: venue-paid platform fee on wrapped notional. The trader's credit comes from the market — never from you.
  • One-week pilot: capped, instrumented, free — with production terms agreed up front.

For their traders

  • A hard floor: beyond the floor strike, losses stop. Leverage with a defined worst case.
  • Paid to be protected: a credit up front, vesting daily — everywhere else, a floor costs premium.
  • Nothing to learn or move: no options interface, no custody, no bridging. The position stays exactly where it is.
  • Leave anytime: toggle off, keep what's vested.
