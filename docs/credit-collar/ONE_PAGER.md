## What it is

Atticus generates **real, delta-hedged perp volume whose costs are paid by options credit, not incentive budgets**. Each position is a genuine, collateralized perp leg on a venue, wrapped in a short-tenor (24h) asymmetric collar (short call / long put) hedged on an options venue. The collar's net credit covers trading fees, funding, and slippage — so the flow has its own economic engine and doesn't leave when a rewards program ends.

Two lanes:

1. **Principal flow** — Atticus opens the positions. Portfolio-level delta neutral; venue-level always one-sided (the neutral pair spans two venues, so the facility is structurally incapable of self-matching on any single book).
2. **Venue-embedded product** — the venue's own traders wrap positions they already hold: they collect the credit (typically covering their fees) and get a defined floor. Organic, sticky volume for the venue; pricing + hedging run behind Atticus's API for an operational fee.

## The problem it solves

Exchanges buy volume today with emissions and rebates, and the flow evaporates when incentives normalize — the post-airdrop volume collapses across the category are public record. Volume with an internal funding engine — auditable, collateralized, incentive-independent — does not currently exist as a supply. That is the product.

Risk automation is live in the same tape: a regime gate (trailing + live signal with hysteresis) throttles or pauses opens in elevated conditions, and a lock-watcher permits early unwinds only when the hedge buyback cost is at or below unvested credit ("never underwater").

## The four questions we tell every venue to ask a volume partner

1. **What funds the flow?** Options credit generated by the structure itself — not your incentive budget.
2. **What's at risk?** Real collateral, real margin, real liquidation risk on every leg.
3. **Can it self-match?** No — neutrality spans venues; every book sees a genuine one-sided position.
4. **Is it auditable?** Every leg logged; settlements oracle-signed; leg-by-leg economics available on request.

## Execution readiness

Full stack built and demo-validated end to end against OKX: block RFQ with order-book fallback, reduce-only IOC unwind rails, partner decision gates, collateral ledger, vesting, incident runbook. Venue adapters are thin modules — additional perp venues (Hyperliquid, dYdX, GRVT, etc.) are additive work, not new architecture.

## The ask

- **Venues:** one or two design partners for the embedded product; standard API access for principal flow — no incentives requested, we earn the fee tier like any real participant.
- **Capital:** a small collateral facility to run the principal pilot ($10k venue deposit + perp margin + buffer). The capital sits as exchange collateral behind fully hedged positions; bounded downside by construction.
