Protection inside the venue's app. One tap, paid today.

A user holding a position on a prediction market taps Lock in. The position now ends at a floor or a cap, and part of it is paid in cash today. Atticus prices it and hedges it on the other side of the same market. The venue keeps the flow, the ledger and the customer; Atticus never holds funds.

Parlays: the lock at the last pick

A parlay is a long shot when bought; no honest floor can be priced on it. Once every pick but one has hit, it is a position on one game at one game's odds, which is what the layer prices. So the lock appears at the last pick and nowhere before. Since Oct 08: 16 tickets, 8 in play, 8 busted on an early pick; the first lock prices when a ticket reaches its last pick.
