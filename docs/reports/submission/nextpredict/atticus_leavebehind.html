Why a venue adds the button

A third choice beside hold and cash out

Cash out sells the whole position at the spread. The lock keeps it: a floor, upside to a cap, cash today.

Revenue with no outcome risk

A published take on every protected position, shared with you. Our P&L equals that take whoever wins.

Integration in days

One JSON quote in, one receipt out. Your brand, your ledger, your official result. Nothing custodial.

Parlays

A parlay is a long shot when bought; no honest floor can be priced on it. Once every pick but one has hit it is a position on one game at one game's odds, which is what the layer prices. So the lock appears at the last pick and nowhere before, and the record shows lock against sell against hold on every ticket that got there.
