## After the meeting, same day

1. Send the slate link and the leave-behind PDF to the one person who asked, with two sentences and no attachment beyond the PDF.
2. Log the meeting here and in the crib sheet's "watch for" column if something changed.
3. If they named a date, put a reminder two days before it.
4. If they asked a number the record does not show, decide whether it belongs on the record or in the data room before writing it down anywhere.
