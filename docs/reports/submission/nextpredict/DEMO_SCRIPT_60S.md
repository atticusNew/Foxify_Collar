# The 60-second phone demo

Open before the conversation starts, phone in hand, screen brightness up. One link, in their brand:

    https://event.earnandprotect.xyz/?brand=DraftKings

(`?brand=Underdog`, `?brand=Novig`, `?brand=ProphetX`, `?brand=PrizePicks` for the others. Sportsbook brands hide the crypto strikes by themselves.)

Say the three words first, then let the screen talk. Do not explain the hedge unless asked.

## 0:00 to 0:10, the positions screen

> "This is your positions screen. Every row is a position a user already holds. The only thing we add is this button."

Point at **Lock in** on the first row. Do not tap yet.

## 0:10 to 0:25, the sheet

Tap **Lock in** on an NFL row.

> "Two endings and the cash now. If it loses, sixty-seven dollars instead of zero. If it wins, a hundred and six instead of a hundred and fifty. Four dollars paid now. Priced live off the public books this second, locked at the tap."

The numbers on the sheet will differ from these; read whatever the sheet shows. The shape is always the same: floor, cap, cash now.

## 0:25 to 0:35, the receipt

Tap **Lock in · +$x now**. Wait for the sheet to close and the cash pulse beside the balance.

> "Cash moved. Receipt in the activity tab. Your ledger, your customer, your official result. We never hold funds."

## 0:35 to 0:50, parlays

Tap **Parlays**. Find a ticket at its last pick.

> "A parlay is a long shot when it is bought; nobody can price an honest floor on it. Once every pick but the last has hit, it is one game at one game's odds. That is where the lock appears, and nowhere before. Three choices at that moment: lock, sell, hold. The record shows all three on every ticket that got there."

## 0:50 to 1:00, the record

Switch to the record: `atticus-event-shadow.onrender.com`. Top card.

> "Seven hundred and thirty-nine settled since September 12. Our P&L equals the published take to the cent, so we carry no outcome risk on the result. Every row is public in integer cents. Your name goes on a slate cut to your leagues; the link is yours alone."

Close:

> "Non-exclusive pilot, revenue share on the published take, nothing up front, integration in days. The one-pager has the numbers. Who should I send the slate link to?"

## If they ask

- **"Who is on the other side?"** "A public book, leg for leg, the second the user taps. The record's identity card is the proof: P&L equals take, whoever won."
- **"What does it cost the user?"** "The credit is net of our take. Realized cost of protection on settled games is 1.3% of stake; the quote says the expected cost before the tap."
- **"Is this custody?"** "No. You pay the credit on your ledger, we true up at settlement. The user's money never touches us."
- **"Why not build it ourselves?"** "You could. The pricing, the hedge, the identity test and the record took a year. Pilot it in days and decide with your own numbers."
- **"Can we have it exclusively?"** "League exclusivity for ninety days is in the term sheet as an option. Venue exclusivity is not."

Do not volunteer: hedge venue names, dealer conversations, netting, anything that ships next.
