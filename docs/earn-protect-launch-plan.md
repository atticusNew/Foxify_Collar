## Partner outreach (parallel track, ~2–3 hrs/week)

Lenders, wallets/trackers, and newer exchanges run on their own playbook — cadence, role-targeting rules, three one-pagers, and full email sequences (initial + 2 follow-ups per audience, plus the intro-ask reply for off-target responders and the Variantial re-engage) live in **`docs/earn-protect-partner-outreach.md`**. Monday batch of ~10 sends, Thursday follow-ups, Friday log. Every send carries the live link and real dashboard numbers.

## Phase 2 — React (Days 7–14)

- **Daily, non-negotiable:** one receipts post on X (cycle concluded, credit paid, knockout re-armed, or an honest refusal — screenshots from the dashboard/app). This compounds; missing days kills it.
- **Weekly revision rule:** any channel at zero response after 2 touches in 7 days → deprioritize; put the time into whichever channel produced a wallet. Any channel that produced ≥1 wallet → double the effort there.
- **Press trigger:** do NOT pitch press at launch. Pitch when one of: 50-wallet cohort full, $25k+ wrapped, or a KOL post pops. The pitch is the traction number, not the product.
- **Capital trigger:** the moment ANY capital lands (even $2–3k scraped) → run the live week immediately: margin probe day 1, live wraps all week, calibration report at the end. Live receipts retro-fuel every channel above.

## Experiments backlog (run one at a time, 2–3 days each, keep what moves wallets)

1. **Founding-slot scarcity posts** — "37/50 slots left" with the dashboard screenshot. Measure: wraps per post.
2. **Refusal transparency** — post a refusal screenshot: "our product declined to sell you protection today because the market couldn't fund it. That's the point." Measure: replies/quotes.
3. **Wrap-a-whale content** — wrap a large public wallet's position in the demo (positions are public data; label clearly as demo) and post the coverage math. Measure: impressions + profile clicks.
4. **1:1 demo calls** — offer 15-min walkthroughs to anyone who replies. Measure: call→wallet conversion.
5. **HL-native payout teaser** — poll: "would you rather be paid on Arbitrum or straight into your HL account?" (we can build HL-native). Measure: engagement + it's real product research.
6. **Bot-first CTA** — one post where the ONLY link is the Telegram bot. Measure: /starts vs web clicks that day.
