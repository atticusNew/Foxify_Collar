## Atticus LinkedIn page post

*Company voice. Link to the Medium article in the first comment or, if link previews are wanted, in the body; LinkedIn down-ranks body links less than it used to, so either works.*

Two days, five stages, about 200 speakers. We wrote down what the prediction-market industry said it is missing, and set it beside what Earn & Protect has running.

Bank of America: $28bn of weekly volume, up nearly 30x in a year. Polymarket's 2024 cohort data, cited from the stage: half of new wallets active at two months, 17% at three. Wedbush, Crypto.com and Sharp Alpha: everyone is posting all the money, and market makers are funding collateral with structured notes. Susquehanna and Cantor: the hedge is the institutional use case, and it is still priced bespoke. White Swan: combination contracts carry $18bn of risk a week, heading for $30bn by Christmas. Sporttrade, Novig, DraftKings and Smarkets: protection is coming, by rule or by reckoning.

Each of those is a description of a position that can only be won or lost.

Earn & Protect changes the shape of the position the holder already has. A floor on the losing side. A credit, paid now, for a reduced payout on the winning side. One tap, on their venue, in their account. We build the hedge in our own account on a regulated venue, route it to the cheaper book, and write the whole thing to a public record with the venue's own order id on it.

The paper quotes the stage by name and session, lists the summit's own forecasts, and says where we stand against each. It is written to be checked.

## Paste procedure for Medium

1. Open `nextpredict_2026_paper_medium.html` in a browser.
2. Select all, copy.
3. In Medium, New story, paste into the body. Headings, italics, bold, blockquote-free inline quotes, lists and links carry across. The horizontal rules carry as Medium section breaks.
4. Medium takes the first H1 as the title and the first H2 as the subtitle. Confirm both, then delete the duplicate byline line if Medium has already attached your profile as author.
5. Add a cover image (a photo from the summit floor or the Atticus wordmark on a plain field). Without one, the LinkedIn preview card is text-only.
6. Publish settings: add the five tags, keep "Allow curators to recommend" on, publish under the Atticus publication if one exists, otherwise under the founder profile.
7. After publishing, copy the story URL into the LinkedIn post above and into the first comment.

## Before publishing

- The 1.89% against 3.15% hedge-cost comparison is read from the current shadow. Re-check the number on the day of posting and update the paper if it has moved.
- "Live on a perpetuals venue" and "running on a public record across event-contract venues" describe deployment on the date of writing. Keep them true or soften them.
- Speakers are named with the title and session given in the source. If any speaker asks not to be quoted, remove the name and leave the session; the argument does not depend on the names.
