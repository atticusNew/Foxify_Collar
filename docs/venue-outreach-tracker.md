# Venue outreach tracker (Sep 2026 refresh)

*Companion to `earn-protect-partner-outreach.md` (templates, cadence, language rules live there). This file is the working list: who to hit, why, how, and where each thread stands. One row per venue. Update `Sent` and `Status` every Friday. A CSV twin (`venue-outreach-tracker.csv`) is kept for Sheets.*

**How to use**

- Monday batch: 10 rows. Pull from Reach **A** first, then **B**. Never more than two **C** rows in a batch.
- `Contact` names marked **(verify)** were not confirmed on this pass. Confirm role and channel before sending; people move.
- Role beats rank: for anything over ~15 people, aim for head of product / derivatives / growth, and use the CEO name only as the anchor to find them.
- `Status` values: `not contacted` · `sent` · `replied` · `call` · `pilot` · `parked` · `prior list` (already touched before this refresh; mark these first).
- Three touches then park (T1, +4d, +10d). Each row's `Sent` holds the T1 date; notes hold the rest.

**Reach key**: **A** realistic (young, needs a differentiator, API or builder-code friendly) · **B** stretch (mid-size, has a BD process) · **C** long shot (large or regulated, slow, but the logo would matter)

**Pitch per class** (one line, then the live link)

- Perp venue / HL frontend: "One toggle: a hard floor under any position and a credit paid to the trader. Priced live from listed options, hedged both legs. Four JSON endpoints, your brand."
- Spot venue / wallet: "Same toggle on spot holdings: floor under BTC/ETH balances, credit paid weekly. Users open the app to check the credit."
- Prediction venue: "Cash-out as a service: we price and hedge a floor under any position; your venue executes and settles. One quote in, one receipt out."
- US-regulated: "Retention product that carries no new risk book for the exchange. Kraken said retail options are a design problem; this is the design."

- **Market makers as hedge counterparties and referrers**: Wintermute, GSR (via Katana), Flow Traders, Amber, Auros, Keyrock. They want options flow; we are options flow by construction. Ask for a quoting relationship, then an intro to the venues they make markets on.

- **Lenders and margin desks**: playbook §2. Unchanged.
- **Chain foundations with grant or partner programs that want perp activity**: Ink, Monad, Base, Solana Foundation, Katana. A grant plus a venue intro in one conversation.
