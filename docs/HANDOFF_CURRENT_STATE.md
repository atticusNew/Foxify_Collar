## 1. Product (what it is)

Earn & Protect: one-tap protection for Hyperliquid perp positions. A WEEKLY plain collar behind a toggle (written to the nearest listed Friday at least 3 days out; `EP_TENOR=daily` opts out): hard floor ~6% below spot (buy put), and the cap (sell call) picked by the CAP RULE (`capRule.ts`: the most credit among listed strikes the market gives at most 20% odds of finishing beyond this cycle; fallback the widest that pays) placed far, not rich; net premium paid to the holder as a CREDIT each cycle (weekly: measured $2.71 vs $0.15 daily on 0.03 BTC, same floor, one hedge round-trip a week instead of seven). Credit vests through the cycle, pays automatically at cycle close. THE CAP (`EP_CAP_MODE=plain`, the default since Sep 20): the cap is the price of the floor — the cap leg is held to expiry, hedged leg for leg, so gains above the cap during the cycle are handed back at the close, netted against the credit (if they exceed it the difference SETTLES FROM GAINS — recorded on the record, gates new protection for that account until settled, `/api/admin/owed/settle`; the retail word is "settles from gains", never "owed"). A cap touch closes NOTHING: it is recorded (`cap_touched`) and the app shows the holder a live RELEASE price — buying the cap leg back on OKX at the ask (`POST /api/release`, partial releases allowed) — so the trader decides, in dollars, whether to keep a rally; after a release every gain is theirs and the floor stays. `EP_CAP_MODE=knockout` is the previous rule (touch ends the cycle, the writer buys the cap leg back at the money, vested credit paid, re-arm) — measured NEGATIVE EV for the writer at any credit a trader would accept, so it is off; the code stays for comparison and the shadow replays the same ledger under either rule. One shape, no tiers (tiers were tried and removed). The cap-distance guard refuses only in knockout mode (2% daily / 4% weekly); in plain mode nothing is refused on distance. Notional fee floor (`EP_MIN_TAKE_BPS`) flag-guarded. RETIRED 2026-09-20 (item 6): `EP_CREDIT_MUST_CLEAR_FUNDING` (funding is the perp's cost, disclosed, never a refusal) and `EP_PRETOUCH_WORKING_ORDER` (nothing rests on the venue); both ignored if set. Card hero is size-aware: the floor leads when the credit is under $10 or 5 bps of notional. Read-only: no keys, no deposits; positions read from HL public API; payouts only to the holder's address. When the market can't fund an honest credit, the engine refuses. Revenue: published 20% of credits sourced (10% founding, first 50 wallets); our take is our ONLY P&L. Hedged, not carried: both legs are filled at open and held; the cap watch prices the cap leg live per wrap (that is the release quote; in knockout mode it is the writer's margin reserve). Atticus is a fee business, never a prop desk (locked rebuttal: "a prop desk takes risk to make money; we charge a fee to remove it"). Copy rule: "You never owe anything" is RETIRED — the honest line is "gains above the cap pay for the floor; release it any time at the live price".

## 3. Hard rules

- **P&L IDENTITY RULE (2026-09-20).** Any time a structure changes who keeps a payoff, re-derive the P&L identity from scratch (writer / trader / venue; expiry and every early exit) and write it into the module docstring and test before anything ships. Origin: the knockout collar let the trader keep the call's premium AND its payoff, so the writer had sold the call for nothing; measured negative-EV live. Book management, netting and re-arming change variance and friction, never that expectation.
- **DISTRIBUTION DECISION (2026-09-20).** The plain collar settles two ways (the cap excess is the floor's premium, due out of the week's gain), and Hyperliquid read-only / agent wallets are a one-way rail (we can pay the holder, never be paid). So the PRODUCT is exchange-integrated: it runs on a partner exchange's ledger where the cap excess nets from the position's PnL at close, disclosed like funding. Hyperliquid stays the public demo, funnel and shadow track record; there the excess is RECORDED (not collected) and gates the next wrap. Never build a custodial rail, never collect from a trader by message, never run the knockout on our balance sheet.
- **CAP RULE (2026-09-20).** `capRule.ts`: the cap is the paying strike with the MOST credit whose market-implied odds of finishing beyond it (the strike's own OKX mark vol, zero drift) are at most `EP_CAP_EXCEED_PROB` (default 0.20, "about one week in five"); fallback the widest paying strike when none qualifies or no vol is readable (`EP_CAP_RULE=widest` restores the old rule). It changes WHICH strike, never who keeps a payoff (identity re-derived in the module docstring). The shadow's cap ladder prints predicted vs realized past-cap % per rung — check the rule against that column before moving the default.
- **EARLY CLOSE / UNWIND QUEUE (2026-09-20).** `execution/unwindQueue.ts`, `EP_UNWIND_QUEUE=1` (plain mode, live OKX lanes): the holder's settlement is decoupled from our execution. They settle at the book's mark-to-market the moment they close; OUR legs go on a queue and are worked passive first (post-only at the mid for `EP_UNWIND_PASSIVE_MS`, re-priced as the mid walks), then IOC via the venue-truth-first ladder — every step a receipt on the wrap record, realized-vs-book kept as `executionEdgeUsdc`. A new wrap that needs the same instruments on the opposite side takes them from the queue at the mid (recycle, full-or-nothing, no fees either side). A leg that will not fill is OUR problem (alert + operator), never a refusal to the holder. Off, the previous IOC-first path runs.
- **RETAIL FIRST (2026-09-20).** The demo is the embedded product for a retail trader, as close to the exchange-integrated version as a standalone can be. `EP_SKIN=retail` (default): every non-admin JSON response passes through `retailView.ts` — hedge legs, instruments, order ids, gross/take, capital formulas, lane names, rule internals and raw venue errors never leave the server (`RETAIL_HIDDEN_KEYS`; `tests/creditCollarRetailView.test.ts` pins the forbidden substrings). Refusals arrive as ONE retail sentence and code, mapped server-side. The card is passive: floor, credit, cap, "nothing to manage"; no mode badge, no receipt table, no release tap, no funding line (it is in How it works). The founding cohort, the waitlist, the "first 50 wallets" gate, `/public` and `/api/stats` are RETIRED — one published take for every wallet (the functions stay in `capsConfig.ts` only to replay old records). Anything shown to institutions is the same service with `EP_SKIN=institutional`, never a different truth. Dev tools must never yield more than the card says: that is the transparency standard, not less disclosure — everything hidden is in the shadow's operator report and the handoff.
- **FLOOR RULE (2026-09-20).** `floorRule.ts`: the floor depth is a rule over a 5–8% band, evaluated per quote most-protective first. `EP_FLOOR_RULE=fixed` (default) is today's `DEMO_FLOOR_PCT` (6%), one probe, unchanged behaviour; `tightest_paying` writes the shallowest depth whose net credit clears `EP_FLOOR_MIN_CREDIT_BPS` (5) of notional, else the depth that pays most, else refuse. One quote path (`listedQuoteByRule`) serves quote/preview/wrap/renewal. The shadow records each quote's counterfactual pick and the report's Floor depth card tallies picks against the live depth — the default moves on that number, not on taste.
- **VENUE REFERENCE (2026-09-20).** `perpShadow/venueReference.ts`: on every hourly scan the same two legs are read at Deribit and Bullish (public, unsigned) the second OKX quotes them; recorded on the open record, summarized on the operator report's Venue reference card (reads, both-sides, beat-OKX count, average gap in bps of spot).
